How it works.

A straightforward way to see the work, choose the right system, and keep your jobs visible across Monterey County and nearby Monterey Bay communities.

  1. 01

    Quick intro call

    We learn your business, the jobs you want more of, who you want to reach, and what is coming up. That gives us a clear place to start.

  2. 02

    The Proof Shoot

    Start with one active jobsite visit for $750. We deliver 3 to 5 edited clips ready to post, so you can see how your real work comes across before choosing a monthly plan.

  3. 03

    Choose your 90-day system

    Once you have seen the clips, choose the system that fits your jobs and posting needs. If you continue, the full $750 Proof Shoot is credited toward month one of your 90-day plan.

  4. 04

    You build. We show it.

    Weekly Proof gives you edited content delivered ready to post. Local Authority and above add publishing support, with capture and delivery on a steady rhythm.

The Proof Shoot is the first step. The monthly system comes after you have seen the work.
